BETA INFORMATION

Beta Guidelines

Last updated: October 26, 2026. Arcana is an independent community companion for IdleArc. The beta is intended for genuine player use and feedback, not as a disposable test environment.

Real accounts and the Founding role

Beta accounts, published guides/builds, player cards, and saved account builds are intended to continue into the full release. Players who join while Founding Beta Tester enrollment is active receive a permanent account badge. This badge is recognition, not moderator or official Wiki publishing access.

Player-created content

Only publish material you have the right to share. Avoid spam, impersonation, threats, private information, or intentionally inaccurate game mechanics. You remain responsible for content under your player card. Community posts are visible to everyone, including non-members; you can edit and temporarily unpublish your own contributions. Moderators may restrict content. Official Wiki publishing requires a separately approved editor role.

Game accuracy

Calculators, Codex records, guide recommendations, and artwork associations are being improved. Game updates can make reference information outdated. The app is not an official IdleArc game account service, and it cannot guarantee that self-entered character or guild details are verified.

Account safety

Use Google or Discord sign-in through the official redirect flow, protect your provider account, and never share your login session or staff keys. To use both providers with the same Arcana profile, connect the second provider from your signed-in My Arcana page. Account-only saves are private, but shared build links can disclose the build and notes to anyone with the URL.

Beta feedback and service changes

Report bugs through Beta & Feedback, especially unexpected data changes, incorrect calculations, broken buttons, or missing art. Features may be changed, temporarily disabled, or moderated for safety during beta. Permanent account data will not be intentionally cleared as a normal part of launch cleanup.

Contact

For site issues, moderation questions, or account data requests, reach the Arcana team through the community Discord.
