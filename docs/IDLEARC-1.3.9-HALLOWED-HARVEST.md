## Core gameplay rules

- Combat harvest: 5 pumpkins every 6 minutes in combat online/offline, delivered at next kill. One in 40 a 25-pumpkin Golden Pumpkin; 500 harvest pumpkin daily cap shown.
- Missions: 3 daily × 40 = 120.
- First three solo Pumpkin Boss fights: 3 × 20 = 60 daily; daily guild boss = 20.
- Send unique candy recipients: 5 × 10 = 50 daily.
- Predictable-source total ceiling: 500 + 120 + 60 + 20 + 50 = 750 daily, excluding invasions and opened candy.
- Invasion windows 04:00, 12:00, 18:00 local for 90 minutes; Pumpkin King 30, max 40 per invasion.
- Community patch: every milestone +5% XP, gold, drop chance across both realms up to +50%; three milestones pay 100 pumpkins each.
- Great Pumpkin: day 7, 3 faces × eight hours; hit each face once. Each face pays 83 pumpkins + ruby pouch + egg pouch + boss ticket.
- Candy: max 3 free daily (6 on Witching Night), 5 opened daily, 5 received daily, 1 Treat Bag received daily, treat 10–30 pumpkins 70% of the time else 60-minute Hexed name.
- Candy Bowl: 300 Arcstone, guild-wide boss ticket, once per guild per day.
- Treat Bag: 100 Arcstone, gives 2 boss tickets and 3 dungeon tickets; prepaid ones from Track are free to send.
- Hallowed Track: ten steps free, Premium 1,200 Arcstone second reward, and every 600 pumpkins after step 10 grants a selectable cache (Premium extra cache). Spending does not reverse track progress.
- Only harvested pumpkins can purchase resources; bonus pumpkins from premium products can only buy specified cosmetics and do not convert.
- Unspent harvested pumpkins convert to pouches at 2× shop pricing after the shop closes.
- One Halloween hero/account, event move once/day.
- Yesterday catch-up costs 2 pumpkins per Arcstone; one free day with general Premium subscription.
