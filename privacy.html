BETA INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026. This notice explains Arcana's beta account features and the information they use. Please review it before using an account.

Information we use

Arcana uses Discord OAuth2 sign-in to verify your identity. It stores a Discord account identifier, the name Discord provides, an Arcana account ID, joining date, and private session records. Arcana does not ask for or store your Discord password or OAuth access token after sign-in.

You may optionally enter an in-game character name, guild, favorite class, short description, raid/guild availability, and a profile picture. You can also save Build Studio plans and publish community builds or guides. Beta bug reports may include an optional email address if you choose to share one. Your profile and community contributions are public; account-only Build Studio saves and session records are private.

How data is used

Arcana uses this information to maintain your account and Founding Beta Tester award, show player cards and authored guides/builds, restore saved plans across devices, moderate contributions, and fix beta issues. An in-game name or guild is self-reported and is not proof of in-game ownership.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored on the Arcana service hosted with Railway. Discord provides authentication, and other linked tools such as ArcanaLFG may have separate infrastructure. Arcana does not currently sell account information to advertisers. External links follow their own privacy rules.

Basic website traffic statistics

Arcana counts page openings and approximate browsing sessions using first-party, anonymous browser-session identifiers. This allows Admins to see daily traffic, popular sections, and an estimate of currently active sessions. The traffic service does not store IP addresses, Discord IDs, user agent strings, external referrers, or full URLs. Session identifiers are randomly generated in session storage, which normally lasts until the tab or browser session ends, and they are hashed by day before storage. Daily aggregate statistics are retained for up to 90 days; session-deduplication hashes are discarded after approximately two days. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings are respected. Analytics begin only when the feature is deployed and cannot reconstruct visits from before its installation. The statistics are private to authorized Admins and the Creator.

Sessions and safety

Arcana uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie with a typical 14-day validity. Short-lived login verification cookies protect the Discord sign-in flow. The beta feedback form uses temporary rate limiting to reduce abuse. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in public profiles, guides, builds, or bug reports.

Persistence and deletion

Beta accounts are intended to continue into the public launch. Founding Beta Tester recognition, once granted, is permanent unless your account is deleted. Domain changes may require signing in again, but should not replace your account records. The service is still in beta, and while persistence is the goal, no online service can guarantee zero data loss. For changes to or removal of your information, use the Arcana community Discord to contact the site team; a self-service deletion screen is not yet available.

Updates

Privacy practices and this notice may change when account features expand. Material changes should be communicated through the website or community before wider launch.
